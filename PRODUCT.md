# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of local Australian businesses with no website: cafés, restaurants, bakeries, salons, tradies (plumbers, roofers, mechanics) and other shops. They get a cold email from Charles at Kerbside Web with a link to a free preview site already built for their business, usually opened on a phone between jobs or service. Their customers are locals who want to call, book, or find hours and the address.

## Product Purpose
Kerbside Web builds a working preview website for a business before asking for money. The owner pays AUD $299 one-off only if they want it live (optional $29/month hosting and small updates). Success is the owner replying "yes" and their customers calling or booking from the site.

## Positioning
Preview first, pay after approval. The preview must read as a site made for that specific business and trade, look worth more than $299, and make the customer action (call, book, get directions) obvious immediately.

## Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS generated from `tools/template.html.j2` and JSON specs; served by GitHub Pages with relative paths.
- Photos are licensed (Wikimedia Commons) stand-ins with credits until the owner sends their own; never copy a business's photos, logo or wording.
- Only facts from public listings (name, address, phone, hours, trade); never invent reviews, awards, years in business or prices the business has not published.
- Previews are noindex and labelled as a preview.

## Brand commitments
- Each trade must look different from the others: a café must not look like a plumber.
- Avoid the generic or AI-template look, and avoid plain or boring.
